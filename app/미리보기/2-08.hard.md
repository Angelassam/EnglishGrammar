## 객관식 (알맞은 말 고르기) (6)

**1.** 우리말과 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / 학생들뿐만 아니라 그들을 가르치는 선생님도 그 여행에 참가할 예정이다. / Not only the students but also the teacher who teaches them ____ going to join the trip.  
① is  
② are  
③ am  
④ be  
⑤ were  
> **정답** ① is  
> **해설** not only A but also B가 주어이면 동사는 B(the teacher)에 맞춰요. 관계대명사절(who teaches them)에 속지 말고 단수 is를 써요.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**2.** 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / The coach as well as the players ____ been practicing since early morning.  
(선수들뿐만 아니라 코치도 이른 아침부터 연습하고 있다.)  
① has  
② have  
③ having  
④ is  
⑤ are  
> **정답** ① has  
> **해설** B as well as A는 앞에 있는 B(the coach)에 동사를 맞춰요. 단수라서 현재완료의 has been을 써요.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**3.** 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / He is good at not only cooking dinner but also ____ the house afterwards.  
(그는 저녁을 요리하는 것뿐만 아니라 그 후에 집을 청소하는 것도 잘한다.)  
① cleaning  
② to clean  
③ clean  
④ cleans  
⑤ cleaned  
> **정답** ① cleaning  
> **해설** A와 B는 같은 모양이어야 해요. 전치사 at 뒤의 A가 동명사 cooking이라서 B도 동명사 cleaning을 써요.  
> 근거: 1. A와 B는 같은 모양으로 (병렬 구조)

**4.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / Not only the price but also the design of this phone surprised me. / = The design ____ the price of this phone surprised me.  
① as well as  
② not only  
③ but also  
④ either  
⑤ neither  
> **정답** ① as well as  
> **해설** not only A but also B = B as well as A예요. 강조되는 B(the design)가 앞으로 오고 그 뒤에 as well as A가 와요.  
> 근거: 2. = B as well as A

**5.** 다음 중 어법상 **옳은** 문장을 고르세요.  
① Not only my parents but also my little brother was surprised at the news.  
② Not only my parents but also my little brother were surprised at the news.  
③ My brother as well as my parents were surprised at the news.  
④ She not only sings well but also dance beautifully.  
⑤ He can neither swim or skate, so he stays home in summer.  
> **정답** ① Not only my parents but also my little brother was surprised at the news.  
> **해설** not only A but also B가 주어이면 B(my little brother)에 맞춰 was를 써요. B as well as A도 B(my brother)에 맞춰 was, A와 B는 같은 모양(sings/dances), neither는 nor와 짝이에요.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**6.** 대화의 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 순서대로 고르세요. / A: Can your grandfather use a smartphone or a computer? / B: No. He can use ____ a smartphone ____ a computer, so he still writes letters.  
(B: 아니. 그는 스마트폰도 컴퓨터도 사용하지 못하셔서 여전히 편지를 쓰셔.)  
① neither, nor  
② either, or  
③ not only, but also  
④ both, and  
⑤ neither, or  
> **정답** ① neither, nor  
> **해설** 'A도 B도 아닌'은 neither A nor B로 써요. 대답이 No라서 either A or B나 both A and B는 뜻이 맞지 않아요.  
> 근거: 4. 같이 알아두면 좋은 상관접속사

## 객관식 (어법상 틀린 것 고르기) (3)

**1.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① Not only the books but also the desk in the classroom were moved yesterday.  
② Tom as well as his classmates is going to the museum tomorrow.  
③ Not only I but also my friends are interested in the science project.  
④ Both the key and the wallet were found under the sofa.  
⑤ He likes not only reading novels but also writing short stories.  
> **정답** ① Not only the books but also the desk in the classroom were moved yesterday.  
> **해설** not only A but also B가 주어이면 동사는 B(the desk)에 맞춰야 해요. → Not only the books but also the desk in the classroom was moved yesterday.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**2.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① She wants not only to travel abroad but also learning new languages.  
② The restaurant is popular not only with students but also with office workers.  
③ You can neither eat nor drink in the school library.  
④ Not only Jane but also her cousins have visited Jeju since 2020.  
⑤ You can choose either the red one or the blue one.  
> **정답** ① She wants not only to travel abroad but also learning new languages.  
> **해설** A와 B는 같은 모양이어야 해요. A가 to부정사(to travel)라서 B도 to부정사로 써요. → She wants not only to travel abroad but also to learn new languages.  
> 근거: 1. A와 B는 같은 모양으로 (병렬 구조)

**3.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① My uncle can play not only the guitar but also he plays the drums.  
② My sister as well as I was late for the first class this morning.  
③ Not only the teacher but also the students were tired after the long trip.  
④ He is famous not only in Korea but also in Japan and China.  
⑤ Both my parents and my grandmother are coming to the school festival.  
> **정답** ① My uncle can play not only the guitar but also he plays the drums.  
> **해설** not only A but also B에서 A와 B는 같은 모양이어야 해요. A가 명사(the guitar)인데 B에 절이 왔어요. → My uncle can play not only the guitar but also the drums.  
> 근거: 1. A와 B는 같은 모양으로 (병렬 구조)

## 빈칸 채우기 (3)

**1.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 쓰세요. (세 단어) / Not only the teacher but also the students were tired after the long trip. / = The students ____ the teacher were tired after the long trip.  
(선생님뿐만 아니라 학생들도 긴 여행 후에 피곤했다.)  
> **정답** as well as  
> **해설** not only A but also B는 B as well as A로 바꿔요. 강조되는 B(the students)가 앞으로 와요.  
> 근거: 2. = B as well as A

**2.** 빈칸에 알맞은 be동사를 현재형으로 쓰세요. / Not only his parents but also he ____ learning Spanish these days.  
(그의 부모님뿐만 아니라 그도 요즘 스페인어를 배우고 있다.)  
> **정답** is  
> **해설** not only A but also B가 주어이면 동사는 B(he)에 맞춰요. 그래서 is를 써요.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**3.** 괄호 안의 말을 알맞은 형태로 바꿔 빈칸에 쓰세요. / I'm interested in not only drawing pictures but also ____ music. (make)  
(나는 그림을 그리는 것뿐만 아니라 음악을 만드는 것에도 관심이 있다.)  
> **정답** making  
> **해설** A(drawing)가 동명사라서 B도 같은 모양인 동명사 making을 써요.  
> 근거: 1. A와 B는 같은 모양으로 (병렬 구조)

## 틀린 부분 고쳐 쓰기 (3)

**1.** Not only my friends but also I is going to the concert tonight.  
(내 친구들뿐만 아니라 나도 오늘 밤 콘서트에 갈 것이다.)  
> **정답** is → am  
> **해설** not only A but also B가 주어이면 동사는 B(I)에 맞춰요. is → am  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**2.** My sister as well as my parents are planning to visit Busan this summer.  
(내 부모님뿐만 아니라 내 언니도 이번 여름에 부산을 방문할 계획이다.)  
> **정답** are planning → is planning  
> **해설** B as well as A는 앞에 있는 B(my sister)에 동사를 맞춰요. are planning → is planning  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**3.** He enjoys not only playing soccer but also to watch it on TV.  
(그는 축구를 하는 것뿐만 아니라 TV로 그것을 보는 것도 즐긴다.)  
> **정답** to watch → watching  
> **해설** A와 B는 같은 모양이어야 해요. A가 동명사(playing)라서 B도 동명사로 써요. to watch → watching  
> 근거: 1. A와 B는 같은 모양으로 (병렬 구조)

## 배열·영작 (3)

**1.** 지난주에 그 소식은 학생들뿐만 아니라 선생님들도 놀라게 했다.  
[ Last week, / the news / surprised / not only / the students / but also / the teachers ]  
> **정답** Last week, the news surprised not only the students but also the teachers. / The news surprised not only the students but also the teachers last week.  
> **해설** 'A뿐만 아니라 B도'는 not only A but also B로 짝을 맞춰 목적어 자리에 써요. 강조되는 B(the teachers)가 뒤에 와요.  
> 근거: 핵심 개념

**2.** 선생님뿐만 아니라 학생들도 지금 그 축제를 즐기고 있다.  
[ the students / as well as / the teacher / be / enjoying / the festival / now ] — 주어진 말을 이용하고, 필요하면 단어를 더하거나 형태를 바꾸세요.  
> **정답** The students as well as the teacher are enjoying the festival now. / Now the students as well as the teacher are enjoying the festival. / Now, the students as well as the teacher are enjoying the festival.  
> **해설** B as well as A는 앞에 있는 B(the students)에 동사를 맞춰요. 복수라서 be → are로 바꿔요.  
> 근거: 3. 주어로 쓰이면 동사는 B에 맞춰요

**3.** 내 여동생뿐만 아니라 나도 어제 그 영화를 봤다.  
[ not only / my sister / but also / I / watch / the movie / yesterday ] — 주어진 말을 이용하고, 필요하면 단어를 더하거나 형태를 바꾸세요.  
> **정답** Not only my sister but also I watched the movie yesterday. / Yesterday, not only my sister but also I watched the movie. / Yesterday not only my sister but also I watched the movie.  
> **해설** not only A but also B를 주어로 쓰고, yesterday가 있어서 watch를 과거형 watched로 바꿔요.  
> 근거: 핵심 개념
