## 객관식 (알맞은 말 고르기) (6)

**1.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / The box was so heavy that I couldn't carry it to the second floor. / = The box was ____ to the second floor.  
① too heavy for me to carry  
② so heavy for me to carry  
③ heavy enough for me to carry  
④ too heavy that I carry  
⑤ too heavy for me to carry it  
> **정답** ① too heavy for me to carry  
> **해설** so + 형용사 + that + 주어 + can't는 too + 형용사 + to부정사로 바꿔요. to부정사로 바꾸면 that절의 목적어 it은 쓰지 않고, 주어가 달라서 for me를 넣어요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. so ~ that 문장으로 바꾸기

**2.** 우리말과 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / 그녀는 비행기를 놓치지 않기 위해서 새벽 다섯 시에 집을 떠났다. / She left home at five in the morning ____ miss the plane.  
① in order not to  
② in order to not  
③ not in order to  
④ so that not to  
⑤ in order not  
> **정답** ① in order not to  
> **해설** '~하지 않기 위해서'는 in order not to + 동사원형으로 쓰고, not은 to 바로 앞에 와요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 1. in order not to

**3.** 밑줄 친 부분과 바꿔 쓸 수 있는 것을 고르세요. / My brother has been saving money since March **in order to buy** a new camera.  
① so that he can buy  
② so that can buy  
③ too much to buy  
④ enough to buy  
⑤ in order that buy  
> **정답** ① so that he can buy  
> **해설** 목적의 in order to + 동사원형은 so that + 주어 + can + 동사원형으로 바꿔 쓸 수 있어요. so that 뒤에는 반드시 주어가 와야 해요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 1. so that + 주어 + can

**4.** 다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장을 고르세요.  
① The water in the lake was warm enough for the children to swim in.  
② The water in the lake was enough warm for the children to swim in.  
③ The water in the lake was warm enough that the children swim in.  
④ The water in the lake was too warm for the children to swim in it.  
⑤ The water in the lake was warm enough for the children swimming in.  
> **정답** ① The water in the lake was warm enough for the children to swim in.  
> **해설** enough는 형용사 뒤에 와서 형용사 + enough + (for + 목적격) + to부정사 순서로 써요. enough warm은 틀려요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. 주의! enough는 형용사 뒤

**5.** 밑줄 친 to부정사의 쓰임이 나머지 넷과 **다른** 것을 고르세요.  
① My grandfather grew up <u>to become</u> a famous painter in his town.  
② He must be rich <u>to buy</u> a car that costs so much money.  
③ She must be kind <u>to help</u> the old man who was carrying heavy bags.  
④ You must be brave <u>to speak</u> in front of so many people.  
⑤ They must be tired <u>to fall</u> asleep as soon as the movie started.  
> **정답** ① My grandfather grew up <u>to become</u> a famous painter in his town.  
> **해설** 첫 번째는 '자라서 결국 ~가 되었다'라는 결과의 to부정사예요. 나머지는 must be + 형용사 뒤에서 '~하다니'라는 판단의 근거를 나타내요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 2. 결과 · 판단의 근거

**6.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 고르세요. / He was clever enough to solve the puzzle that nobody else could solve. / = He was ____ the puzzle that nobody else could solve.  
① so clever that he could solve  
② so clever that he couldn't solve  
③ too clever to solve  
④ clever so that he solves  
⑤ so clever to solve  
> **정답** ① so clever that he could solve  
> **해설** 형용사 + enough + to부정사는 so + 형용사 + that + 주어 + can(could)으로 바꿔요. 과거 문장이라 could를 쓰고, enough to는 긍정이라 couldn't는 틀려요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. so ~ that 문장으로 바꾸기

## 객관식 (어법상 틀린 것 고르기) (3)

**1.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① The soup was too hot that I couldn't eat it right away.  
② She studied all night in order not to fail the test again.  
③ The boy was strong enough to lift the box that was full of books.  
④ I turned off my phone so that I could focus on my homework.  
⑤ He was too sleepy to finish the book he had borrowed.  
> **정답** ① The soup was too hot that I couldn't eat it right away.  
> **해설** '너무 ~해서 …할 수 없다'는 too ~ to부정사이거나 so ~ that + 주어 + can't예요. 둘을 섞은 too ~ that은 틀려요. → The soup was so hot that I couldn't eat it right away.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. so ~ that 문장으로 바꾸기

**2.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① He was too tired to finish the books that was on his desk.  
② The room is not big enough for all of us to sit in.  
③ I wrote down his number in order not to forget it.  
④ Tom ran fast enough to catch the bus that was leaving.  
⑤ She was too busy to answer the e-mails that she had received.  
> **정답** ① He was too tired to finish the books that was on his desk.  
> **해설** too ~ to부정사 자체는 맞지만, 관계대명사 that의 선행사가 복수 the books라서 동사는 were를 써야 해요. → He was too tired to finish the books that were on his desk.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. too ~ to / enough to

**3.** 다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장을 고르세요.  
① The tea was too hot for me to drink it at once.  
② It was so cold that we couldn't play outside for long.  
③ The movie was interesting enough for the children to watch twice.  
④ He got up at six in order to catch the first train to Seoul.  
⑤ The girl was too shy to say hello to the new students.  
> **정답** ① The tea was too hot for me to drink it at once.  
> **해설** too ~ to부정사로 바꾸면 주어(the tea)와 같은 목적어 it은 쓰지 않아요. → The tea was too hot for me to drink at once.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. too ~ to / enough to

## 빈칸 채우기 (3)

**1.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 쓰세요. (세 단어) / The man was too old to climb the mountain that he loved. / = The man was so old that he ____ the mountain that he loved.  
(그 남자는 너무 나이가 들어서 자신이 사랑했던 그 산을 오를 수 없었다.)  
> **정답** couldn't climb / could not climb  
> **해설** too ~ to는 so ~ that + 주어 + can't로 바꾸는데, 과거 문장이라 couldn't climb으로 써요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. so ~ that 문장으로 바꾸기

**2.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 쓰세요. (네 단어) / I have been practicing every day in order to win the contest. / = I have been practicing every day ____ win the contest.  
(나는 그 대회에서 우승하기 위해 매일 연습해 오고 있다.)  
> **정답** so that I can  
> **해설** 목적의 in order to는 so that + 주어 + can으로 바꿔 쓸 수 있어요. 주절이 현재완료 진행이라 can을 그대로 써요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 1. so that + 주어 + can

**3.** 두 문장이 같은 뜻이 되도록 빈칸에 알맞은 말을 쓰세요. (세 단어) / This bridge is so strong that trucks can cross it safely. / = This bridge is ____ for trucks to cross safely.  
(이 다리는 트럭이 안전하게 건널 수 있을 만큼 충분히 튼튼하다.)  
> **정답** strong enough  
> **해설** so ~ that + 주어 + can은 형용사 + enough + to부정사로 바꿔요. enough는 형용사 뒤에 오고, 주어가 다르니 for trucks를 넣어요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. 주의! enough는 형용사 뒤

## 틀린 부분 고쳐 쓰기 (3)

**1.** The baby was enough old to walk by himself when we visited last year.  
(작년에 우리가 방문했을 때 그 아기는 혼자 걸을 만큼 충분히 나이가 들어 있었다.)  
> **정답** enough old → old enough  
> **해설** enough는 형용사 뒤에 와요. enough old → old enough  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. 주의! enough는 형용사 뒤

**2.** He wore a thick coat in order to not catch a cold in the snow.  
(그는 눈 속에서 감기에 걸리지 않기 위해서 두꺼운 코트를 입었다.)  
> **정답** to not → not to  
> **해설** '~하지 않기 위해서'는 in order not to로 쓰고, not은 to 바로 앞에 와요. to not → not to  
> 근거: Part 2 / 1. in order not to

**3.** The question was so difficult that no one couldn't answer it in class.  
(그 문제는 너무 어려워서 수업 시간에 아무도 답할 수 없었다.)  
> **정답** couldn't → could  
> **해설** no one에 이미 부정의 뜻이 있어서 that절에 couldn't를 또 쓰면 안 돼요. no one could answer it으로 써요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. so ~ that 문장으로 바꾸기

## 배열·영작 (3)

**1.** 그 소년은 너무 어려서 어제 시작된 그 영화를 볼 수 없었다.  
[ too young / the boy / was / to watch / that / yesterday / the movie / started ]  
> **정답** The boy was too young to watch the movie that started yesterday.  
> **해설** too + 형용사 + to부정사 순서로 쓰고, the movie 뒤에 관계대명사 that절을 이어요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. too ~ to / enough to

**2.** 우리는 그 박물관의 그림들을 볼 수 있도록 일찍 떠났다.  
[ we / leave / early / so that / we / can / see / the paintings / in the museum ] — 주어진 말을 이용하고, 필요하면 단어를 더하거나 형태를 바꾸세요.  
> **정답** We left early so that we could see the paintings in the museum.  
> **해설** 목적은 so that + 주어 + can으로 쓰는데, 주절이 과거 left라서 can도 could로 바꿔 시제를 맞춰요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 1. so that + 주어 + can

**3.** 그녀는 작년에 지어진 그 다리를 건널 만큼 충분히 용감했다.  
[ she / be / brave / enough / cross / the bridge / that / build / last year ] — 주어진 말을 이용하고, 필요하면 단어를 더하거나 형태를 바꾸세요.  
> **정답** She was brave enough to cross the bridge that was built last year.  
> **해설** 형용사 + enough + to부정사 순서로 쓰고, 다리는 '지어진' 것이니 관계대명사절 안을 수동태 was built로 써요.  
> 근거: Part 2 / 3. 주의! enough는 형용사 뒤
